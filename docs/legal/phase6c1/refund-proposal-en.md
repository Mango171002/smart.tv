### How the start date is identified (verified in the order system, read-only)

- When our team confirms that a payment has been received, it marks the order as paid in the operations tool
  (« Converti »). At that moment the order system records, with the order number, the **date and time of the payment
  confirmation** (`invoice_issued_at`, set once; it is not changed if the action is undone and done again). Every
  confirmation is also logged with its time in the order history.
- **The date on which the customer actually sent the payment is not recorded.** The payment confirmation date is
  therefore the date on which our team confirmed receipt — never earlier than the payment itself, so it can only
  favour the customer.
- The subscription confirmation email ("Your subscription is now active!") is sent right after this confirmation,
  but **its sending date is not used as the reference**: an email can be delayed, filtered or fail to send. The
  reference is the date recorded in the order records. Today the email shows the order number but **not** the date;
  showing it ("Payment confirmed on …") is recommended in a separate email lot (no email is changed here).

## Proposed policy text

### 1. Definitions

- **Activation**: the moment we give you access to the Service. Activation takes place **before** payment.
- **Payment confirmation date**: the date on which our team confirms that your payment has been received, as recorded
  in our order records together with your order number. It is normally the date of your subscription confirmation
  email, but our order records prevail if the two differ (for example if the email is delayed or not received). You
  can ask our support team for this date at any time.
- **Calendar days**: all days, including weekends and public holidays.

### 2. Free trial first

You can test the Service for free **[24 hours]** on your own device before ordering. Nothing to pay and nothing to
cancel. One free trial per customer.

### 3. Our "Satisfied or refunded" guarantee (commercial guarantee)

3.1 **How it works.** We activate your access first; you pay after activation. If you are not satisfied, you can ask
for a full refund **within 7 calendar days of the payment confirmation date**.

3.2 **Counting the period.** The period starts on the day after the payment confirmation date and ends at 23:59 on
the 7th calendar day **[DECISION-19: reference time zone — e.g. the customer's time zone or UTC]**. A request
received within this period is accepted, even if it is processed after the period ends.

3.3 **Limits.** One refund per customer. The guarantee does not apply to accounts suspended or terminated for a serious
breach of our Terms (for example sharing or reselling access).

3.4 **Relationship with your statutory rights.** This guarantee is voluntary and **in addition to** your statutory
rights (section 5). It does not replace, shorten or limit any right you have under the mandatory law of your country of
residence, including any right of withdrawal.

### 4. Service malfunction

4.1 If the Service does not work for you because of a problem on our side, contact our support team first: most issues
are fixed quickly.

4.2 If our team cannot restore the Service within **[DECISION-12: e.g. 72 hours]** of your report, you can choose
**[DECISION-12: e.g. an extension of your subscription for the period affected, or a refund of the unused part of your
subscription]**.

4.3 Problems caused by your internet connection, your device or a third-party application are not a malfunction of the
Service, but our team will help you as far as possible.

4.4 Your statutory remedies for a digital service that is not as described remain fully applicable. **⚖ Legal
review.**

### 5. Your statutory rights — expressly preserved

5.1 Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have as a consumer under the mandatory law of your country of
residence.

5.2 **Right of withdrawal.** Depending on your country of residence, you may have a statutory right to withdraw from
the contract (for example 14 days for consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom). This right applies in
addition to the 7-day guarantee and under the conditions set by law. When the withdrawal period starts, how it applies
when access is activated before payment and within that period, and whether a proportionate amount may be kept for
the days of use, **will be stated here after legal review for each market**. **⚖ Legal review.** This policy does
**not** state that the right of withdrawal ends when access is activated.

5.3 **Unused part after the 7-day guarantee** (currently published for EU/UK customers: "After the first 7 days, we
refund the unused part of your subscription"): **[DECISION-13 / ⚖ Legal review: keep, extend to all customers, or
replace by the statutory calculation]**.

### 6. How to ask for a refund

6.1 Send your order number to our support email address or on WhatsApp, stating that you want a refund (a short message
is enough). EU/UK consumers can also use the withdrawal form **[⚖ to be provided]**.

6.2 We confirm receipt of your request within **[DECISION: e.g. 2 business days]**.

### 7. How you are refunded

7.1 We refund within **14 days** of accepting your request (current published period).

7.2 We refund using the same payment method you used, where possible. When this is not possible (for example
cash-based money transfers or cryptocurrency), our team agrees another method with you **[DECISION-14: methods,
currency and exchange rate for crypto, who bears transfer fees]**. **⚖ Legal review.**

7.3 The amount refunded is the amount you actually paid for the order **[DECISION: treatment of payment fees and
promotional extras such as "+30 days free"]**.

### 8. After these periods

Subscriptions are not refundable after the periods above, except in case of malfunction (section 4) or where the law
requires it. Subscriptions never renew automatically.
