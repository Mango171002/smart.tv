## Part A — Options for the start of the commercial refund period (to decide)

| Option | Wording | For the customer | For SYGMA TV+ | Points to check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. From activation** (current) | "within 7 days of activation" | Period may be partly used before paying (payment comes after activation) | Simple to track (activation date known) | Inconsistent with "pay after activation": a customer paying on day 6 has 1 day left |
| **B. From payment confirmation** (owner proposal) | "within 7 days of the confirmation of your payment" | Full 7 days after paying | Date = "Converti" email (order number) — easy to prove | Customer may have used the Service for several days before paying |
| **C. From the later of activation and payment confirmation** | "within 7 days of the later of activation and payment confirmation" | Never less than 7 days after paying | Same as B in practice (payment always after activation) | Equivalent to B with the current process; more robust if the process changes |
| **D. Time-boxed from activation, with a minimum after payment** | "within 7 days of activation, and in any case at least [N] days after your payment" | Protects late payers | More complex to explain | — |

Recommendation for discussion: **B** (or C, equivalent today), because the payment confirmation email gives a clear,
provable start date. **[DECISION-11]**

## Proposed policy text (assuming option B — adapt to the decision)

### 1. Free trial first

You can test the Service for free **[24 hours]** on your own device before ordering. Nothing to pay and nothing to
cancel. One free trial per customer.

### 2. Our "Satisfied or refunded" guarantee (commercial guarantee)

2.1 **Eligibility.** If you are not satisfied, you can ask for a full refund **within 7 days of the confirmation of
your payment** (the date of the email confirming that your subscription is active) **[DECISION-11]**.

2.2 **Limits.** One refund per customer. The guarantee does not apply to accounts suspended or terminated for a serious
breach of our Terms (for example sharing or reselling access).

2.3 This guarantee is voluntary. It is in addition to, and does not limit, your statutory rights (section 4).

### 3. Service malfunction

3.1 If the Service does not work for you because of a problem on our side, contact our support team first: most issues
are fixed quickly.

3.2 If our team cannot restore the Service within **[DECISION-12: e.g. 72 hours]** of your report, you can choose
**[DECISION-12: e.g. an extension of your subscription for the period affected, or a refund of the unused part of your
subscription]**.

3.3 Problems caused by your internet connection, your device or a third-party application are not a malfunction of the
Service, but our team will help you as far as possible.

3.4 Your statutory rights regarding non-conforming digital services remain unaffected. **⚖ Legal review.**

### 4. Your statutory rights

4.1 Depending on your country of residence, you may have a statutory right of withdrawal (for example 14 days for
consumers in the European Union and the United Kingdom) and statutory remedies if the Service is not as described.

4.2 How the right of withdrawal applies when access is activated during the withdrawal period — including when the
period starts and whether a proportionate amount may be kept for the days of use — will be set out here after legal
review for each market. **⚖ Legal review.** This proposal does **not** state that the right of withdrawal ends when
access is activated.

4.3 **Unused part after 7 days** (current published rule for EU/UK customers: "After the first 7 days, we refund the
unused part of your subscription"): keep, extend to all customers, or replace by the statutory calculation
**[DECISION-13 / ⚖ Legal review]**.

### 5. How to ask for a refund

5.1 Send your order number to our support email address or on WhatsApp, stating that you want a refund (a short message
is enough). EU/UK consumers can also use the withdrawal form **[⚖ to be provided]**.

5.2 We confirm receipt of your request within **[DECISION: e.g. 2 business days]**.

### 6. How you are refunded

6.1 We refund within **14 days** of accepting your request (current published period).

6.2 We refund using the same payment method you used, where possible. When this is not possible (for example
cash-based money transfers or cryptocurrency), our team agrees another method with you **[DECISION-14: methods,
currency and exchange rate for crypto, who bears transfer fees]**. **⚖ Legal review** (refund fees and methods for
consumers).

6.3 The amount refunded is the amount you actually paid for the order **[DECISION: treatment of payment fees and
promotional extras such as "+30 days free"]**.

### 7. After these periods

Subscriptions are not refundable after the periods above, except in case of malfunction (section 3) or where the law
requires it. Subscriptions never renew automatically.
