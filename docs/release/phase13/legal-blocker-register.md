## L-09 — Historical transaction amount accuracy (code-level evidence only)

**How amounts are stored**
- `contact_requests` (assisted orders) stores the plan of interest — `interest_tier`, `interest_term`,
  `interest_streams` — and, on « Converti », `invoice_number` and `invoice_issued_at`
  (`supabase/migrations/20261110230034_plan_tiers.sql:43-44`, `20261109000633_waitlist_plan_interest.sql:6-8`,
  `20261115110000_orders_repeat_invoices.sql:13-16`). **No migration adds an amount, currency, price ID, payment method
  or tax column to `contact_requests`.** The amount e-mailed is therefore not stored with the request.
- `orders` (automated path, unused while `/api/checkout` is locked) stores `currency`, `subtotal_minor`, `tax_minor`,
  generated `total_minor`, frozen after `draft`, with a trigger requiring `subtotal = server price`
  (`supabase/migrations/20261108134827_customers_orders.sql:34-37`, `:173-199`).
- Prices: `prices` rows with `valid_from` / `valid_to`; the 14 November price change closed old rows instead of deleting
  them (`supabase/migrations/20261114160000_prices_owner_2026_11_14.sql:1-5`); anon reads only current rows
  (`20261108134854_api_privileges_rls.sql:41-47`).

**How the e-mailed amount is produced**
- On « Converti », `sendConversionEmail` recomputes the amount from the **current** price table
  (`src/lib/telegram/ops.ts:417-423`: `getPriceTable()` → `computeQuote()` on the request's tier/term/streams),
  formatted `$X.XX USD` (`templates.ts:71-72`, `:314`). If the lookup fails, the e-mail is sent without an amount
  (`ops.ts:424`). The price table is cached 5 minutes and falls back to the static JSON if the database read fails
  (`src/lib/price-source.ts:9`, `:35-58`).
- The static table, the seed and the 14 November migration contain the same 48 amounts (checked in this phase by
  comparing `src/config/price-tables.json` with `supabase/seed.sql` and
  `20261114160000_prices_owner_2026_11_14.sql`: 0 differences). The live database content was **not** checked.
- History: before migration `20261115110000` (15 November) the conversion e-mail had no amount (DECISIONS D57); the
  current table dates from 14 November (DECISIONS D54).

**Mismatch risks (from code)**
1. The e-mailed « Amount Paid » is the list price at conversion time, not the amount received: discounts, « +30 days
   free », fees, currency conversion, crypto/Western Union/MoneyGram amounts are not reflected.
2. If prices change between order and « Converti », the e-mail shows the new price, while the customer saw the old
   one (the « order received » e-mail shows no price, so the customer has no durable record of it).
3. The amount reflects the plan chosen on the web form; a plan changed on WhatsApp is not reflected.
4. The validator accepts the `lifetime` term (`src/lib/validation.ts:56`) although the site does not offer it
   (Phase 11: `SIM_TERMS` excludes it); lifetime prices exist in the table, so a crafted request could produce a
   « Lifetime » confirmation with an amount.
5. No tax line; « Payment Status: Paid » is asserted by the staff action, not by a payment record.
6. A past e-mail's amount can only be reconstructed from the price row valid at `invoice_issued_at` (possible because
   old rows are kept), not read back.

**Risk:** High.

**Recommended resolution.** Before further sales: record the amount actually received, currency, method and date
when « Converti » is pressed, and show that in the e-mail (Phase 11 option B-3); restrict `term` to offered terms in
order requests. For past conversions: owner (with engineering, under an approved read-only query) reconciles each
e-mailed amount against the payment actually received and the price row valid at `invoice_issued_at`; tax adviser
decides whether corrections must be sent.

**Responsible:** owner (payment records), engineering (schema and e-mail change after approval), tax adviser
(corrective documents).

**Required evidence:** payment records per converted request (amount, currency, method, date); an approved
read-only reconciliation query and its result; tax adviser's view on corrections.

**Blocks prelaunch publication:** No. **Blocks commercial sales:** Yes.
