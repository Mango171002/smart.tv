## Smart TV (Samsung, LG) — `/guides/smart-tv`

- **H1** : Set up SYGMA TV+ on a Samsung or LG Smart TV
- **Meta title** : Set up SYGMA TV+ on Samsung or LG Smart TV | SYGMA TV+
- **Description** : Step-by-step guide to set up SYGMA TV+ on a Samsung or LG Smart TV: network, software update, app store, your access details and troubleshooting.
- **Introduction** : Samsung and LG Smart TVs install apps from their built-in app store. Menus vary slightly by model and year; the labels below are the most common ones.

**Before you start**

- A TV connected to the internet, and its remote.
- The WhatsApp message from our team with your access details and the name of the app to install.
- Use a network cable if you can, or keep the device close to your Wi-Fi router: a stable connection prevents most interruptions.

**Steps**

1. **Check the connection** — Open the TV Settings, then Network, and check that the TV is connected to the internet (cable or Wi-Fi). [VERIFY ON DEVICE]
2. **Update the TV** — In Settings, open Support (or "About this TV") and run the software update if one is offered. An up-to-date TV avoids many app problems. [VERIFY ON DEVICE]
3. **Open the app store** — On Samsung, press Home and open "Apps". On LG, open the app store from the home screen ("LG Content Store" or "Apps", depending on the model). [VERIFY ON DEVICE]
4. **Install the app we told you about** — Search for the app name sent by our team [APP NAME — TO BE CONFIRMED], select it, then choose "Install". Add it to the home screen to find it easily.
5. **Enter your access details** — Open the app and enter the login details received on WhatsApp, exactly as written (upper and lower case).
6. **Check playback** — Start a channel and let it play for a few minutes to check that the picture is smooth.

**Common problems**

- **I cannot find the app in the store** — The store catalogue depends on the TV model and country. Write to us with the brand, model and year of your TV: we will tell you what works, for example a streaming device plugged into the TV.
- **The picture stutters or freezes** — Restart your router and the TV, move the TV closer to the router or use a network cable, then try again.
- **My login details are refused** — Check what you typed (spaces, upper case). If it still fails, send us a photo of the error screen.
