# Phase 7 — 90-day content roadmap

**Proposal.** Dates count from the owner's authorisation (J0). Each item ships only after review and explicit
approval. Volumes: none assumed; progress is measured with indexing and Search Console data (baseline: 5 impressions,
1 click, CTR 20 %, 6 sitemap URLs — too small to steer keyword choices).

## Guiding rules

- Reuse before creating: the 5C Help center (7 topics) and About page already cover devices, trial, ordering, payment,
  troubleshooting, contact and refunds in English.
- Quality over quantity: at most a handful of new pages per month, each with first-hand, verifiable information.
- No promotional content for channels, sports or films without distribution rights.
- Every non-English page is written or adapted by a native speaker, from the validated English (6A/6B wording).

## Explicitly out of scope (90 days)

- Country variants (`fr-ch`, `fr-ca`, `en-gb`…) until prices, currency, law or availability differ.
- Channel lists, sports schedules, competition or film pages.
- Bulk or automatically generated pages.
